Glioblastoma

TGen-led study lays groundwork for monitoring glioblastoma with cell-free DNA

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • The Cancer Letter flagged a TGen-led study on using circulating cell-free DNA (cfDNA) to track glioblastoma.
  • Framed as groundwork, not a validated clinical test.
  • Blood-based monitoring is of interest in GBM because tumor tissue is hard to resample.

Need to know: A liquid-biopsy direction worth watching for brain tumors. Early-stage research, so the open question is how sensitive cfDNA is when the blood-brain barrier limits tumor DNA shedding.

Keto updates

Blood BHB of at least 0.5 mmol/L best predicted weight loss in a 14-day keto study

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • 217 adults with overweight or obesity measured blood ketones and weight daily during a 14-day ketogenic diet.
  • A beta-hydroxybutyrate level of 0.5 mmol/L or higher was the strongest predictor of weight loss.
  • Participants reached that level in 7.7 days on average, and nearly 60% got there between days 5 and 8.
  • Average weight loss was 7.0%.

Need to know: A concrete, measurable ketone threshold from a daily-tracking study. Short duration and a weight-loss population, so it says little about longer-term or therapeutic use.

BHB reduced tau build-up in Alzheimer's models even when cells could not burn it

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • A BHB-boosting diet improved tau handling in a mouse model of tauopathy.
  • In cell and brain-tissue models, BHB reduced tau aggregation and secretion.
  • A form of BHB that cells cannot oxidize gave the same effect, pointing to a signaling role rather than fuel.

Need to know: Adds to evidence that ketones act as signaling molecules in the brain, not just as fuel. Preclinical only.
