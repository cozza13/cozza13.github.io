PH+ ALL: ponatinib plus blinatumomab beat imatinib plus chemo in Phase III NEJM trial

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • GIMEMA ALL2820, Phase III, N=236, published in NEJM 2026.
  • Arm: ponatinib + blinatumomab vs imatinib + chemotherapy.
  • At 24 months: EFS 86% vs 67%; molecular EFS 68% vs 42%; OS 90% vs 85%; relapse 5% vs 13%.

Need to know: Strong chemo-sparing signal in Philadelphia-chromosome-positive ALL. The event-free survival gap is hard to ignore.

Keto updates

Nick Norwitz: gut microbe–made C15:0 saturated fat slowed arterial plaque like a statin in mice

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • 2026 preclinical work: B. uniformis was enriched in healthier hearts vs CVD patients (223 healthy vs 109 CVD).
  • The bacterium produces pentadecanoic acid (C15:0), also found in fatty fish and full-fat dairy such as pecorino.
  • In heart-disease-prone mice, the microbe cut plaque on a scale comparable to atorvastatin.
  • C15:0 inhibited HMG-CoA reductase — the same enzyme statins target.

Need to know: Wild mechanism paper, still preclinical. Interesting for dairy-fat and microbiome debates; not a license to reinterpret lipids. Research interest only.
