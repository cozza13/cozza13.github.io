1. Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Single-cell map of ecDNA in glioblastoma: most tumor cells in one sample carried EGFR and MDM4 circles

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • Posted by co-author @FeiYuan66358; the scCirclehunter paper is in Cell Discovery (Dec 2025), with open code on GitHub.
  • The tool infers which extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) circles each cell carries from single-cell ATAC-seq data.
  • In one GBM patient, 70.4% (336/477) of malignant cells carried ecEGFR plus ecMDM4, and 18% (86/477) carried ecPDGFRA plus ecMDM4.
  • Using ecNR2E1 as an example, the authors show ecDNA driving tumor progression, and suggest a link between ecDNA and more mitochondrial transfer.

Need to know: EGFR amplification in GBM often sits on ecDNA, so copy number can differ from cell to cell; that heterogeneity is one proposed reason EGFR-targeted drugs have struggled. A research method on public datasets, not a treatment (Emerging).

@FeiYuan66358 · Paper · Code

Journal roundup: EPHRIN-B2 CAR-T cells in brain tumor models, and MRI foundation models for GBM survival prediction

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Shinshu University (J Neurooncol): among GBM/gliosarcoma samples, 74% stained strongly for EPHA2 and 37% for EPHB4; high co-expression was concentrated in high-grade tumors.
  • EPHRIN-B2-based CAR-T cells killed all six brain tumor cell lines tested; in two mouse models they reduced tumor signal vs saline at some time points, but not significantly vs control T cells.
  • NCI/Penn (NPJ Precis Oncol): an MRI foundation model trained on 1,007 patients (UPENN-GBM, UCSF-PDGM, TCGA-GBM) reached a C-index of 0.667 vs 0.612 for radiomics.
  • Adding age, extent of resection and MGMT status raised it to 0.691.

Need to know: Both early. The CAR-T is a dual-receptor approach still at the mouse stage, and the AI survival models improve only modestly over older methods.

PubMed 43068431 · PubMed 43069309

Viral "untreatable" pediatric brain cancer post recycles a June phase 1; a NYT DIPG piece circulates

For you 5 | Unique 5

Need to know: A real signal, but small and early, and the viral "breakthrough" framing goes beyond the paper. Cite the paper, not the post.

Nature: blocking mitochondrial protein import makes melanoma more visible to the immune system (mouse)

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • Puigserver lab at Dana-Farber (published Nov 11): depleting TIMM50, part of the TIM23 import complex, made tumors more immunogenic.
  • Mechanism: mitochondrial DNA escapes via mitochondria-derived vesicles, activates cGAS-STING and IFN-beta, and that raises MHC class I antigen presentation.
  • It worked together with immune checkpoint blockade in mouse melanoma models.

Need to know: Preclinical. Another link between tumor mitochondria and immune evasion, a theme that matters for immunologically "cold" tumors like GBM.

@DanaFarber · Nature

Cancer vaccine galinpepimut-S extends survival in a phase 3 AML trial (company topline)

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • SELLAS (Nov 17): the REGAL trial randomized 126 AML patients in second or later remission who could not get a transplant to the vaccine or best available treatment.
  • Median overall survival was 24.0 vs 14.7 months (HR 0.59, two-sided p=0.035) after 80 deaths; side effects were mostly grade 1-2 injection-site reactions.
  • Open-label and not yet peer-reviewed; SELLAS plans a BLA in the first half of 2027.
  • @m_goes_distance (5.4K views) lists it with other recent phase 3 wins and says "we are going to solve cancer in 3-5 years max."

Need to know: A rare phase 3 survival win for a peptide cancer vaccine (it targets WT1, one of the antigens in the pediatric ReMIND trial above). Blood cancer, not brain, and the "3-5 years" line is hype.

2. AI updates

Parmita Mishra: AI solved the cheapest part of drug discovery

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • @parmita (founder/CEO of Precigenetics; 153 likes, 92 bookmarks): molecule design is "close to solved," but "nothing that decides whether a drug works has moved significantly."
  • Drugs fail because they do not work (40-50% of failures) or because they hurt people (30%); she calls both measurement problems, not model problems.
  • Milestones she lists: 15-20 AI-discovered programs at pivotal Phase 3 readouts in 2026-2027; an FDA decision on zasocitinib in Q1 2027, likely to be claimed as the first AI-discovered approval; rentosertib Phase 3 data in Dec 2029.
  • Her answer is data at scale from living cells, which is what her company builds.

Need to know: From the Ai people list: a grounded counterweight to "AI will cure cancer" posts. Note that she sells a measurement platform.

UK starts EDITH, the world's largest AI breast screening trial: nearly 700,000 women

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • NHS England (Nov 23): women at Cambridge University Hospitals are the first invited; 30 UK sites over three years; £10 million, funded by NIHR.
  • Today two specialists read each mammogram; the trial tests whether AI assistance means most images need only one.

3. Keto updates

Bikman: insulin resistance tracks with prostate cancer odds

For you 8 | Unique 6

  • @BenBikmanPhD (Keto list) cites a National Cancer Institute case-control study (JNCI 2003; 128 cases and 306 controls, Chinese men).
  • Top third for insulin resistance (HOMA): odds ratio 2.78 vs the bottom third; top third for insulin sensitivity: 0.35.
  • Men highest in both insulin resistance and waist-to-hip ratio: OR 8.21 (95% CI 2.84-23.70).
  • He frames insulin as a growth signal and notes that many cancer cells carry more insulin receptors than normal cells.

Need to know: Observational and retrospective; the authors said it needs confirmation in prospective studies. Bikman calls it "one large study," but it had 434 men. It fits the insulin-and-cancer thread behind metabolic approaches.

@BenBikmanPhD · PubMed 12509402

Three days of a flipped sleep schedule raised inflammation markers (PNAS)

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • @TheDrMAWZ summarizes a controlled lab study: healthy adults' behavioral and light cycles were inverted by 12 hours for three days.
  • 24-hour IL-6, CRP, resistin and TNF-alpha rose 3% to 29%.
  • Wake-time cardiac vagal modulation (from HRV) fell 8% to 15%, and 24-hour blood pressure rose 3.0/1.5 mmHg.
  • His line: "You can keep your diet clean and still push inflammation up with your clock."

Need to know: Small, short-term lab study (Morris et al., PNAS 2016), but it ties sleep timing to the same HRV and CRP numbers many people track.

@TheDrMAWZ · PubMed 26858430

4. General news

Your data, your apps: the new HIPAA rule makes app access free, while LinkedIn limits exports

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • @Flexpa: "personal health application" is now a defined term in HIPAA, based on whether the data is managed, shared and controlled by or primarily for the individual.
  • The final rule (published Nov 19) bars providers and plans from charging patients for access through a personal health app and cuts the response time to 15 days.
  • It takes effect Jan 19, 2027; compliance is required by July 19, 2027.
  • @nikillinit (47K views): "Linkedin is blocking your data exports in December," and he expects platforms to get more hostile as users do more with their exports.

Need to know: Good news for patients who pull their own records into apps; the opposite trend is showing up on consumer platforms.
