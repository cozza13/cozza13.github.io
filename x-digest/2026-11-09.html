1. Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Personalized neoantigen dendritic-cell vaccine (ZSNeo-DC) in newly diagnosed GBM

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • A phase Ib report describes ZSNeo-DC given after surgery and chemoradiotherapy in 11 patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma.
  • Posts cite median PFS of 16.2 months; median OS not reached after 41.6 months of median follow-up; all 11 were alive at 12 months after surgery in one summary.
  • Neoantigen-specific responses rose (median ~10-fold after five vaccinations; one post cites up to 507-fold), with expansion of new T-cell clones correlating with longer PFS.
  • Only two mild fevers were attributed to vaccination in the summary.

Need to know: Single-arm phase Ib with a very small sample. Immune and survival signals are encouraging but cannot establish benefit without a control arm. Prefer the primary write-up over investor framing.

Scientists filmed DNA moving between human cells

For you 7 | Unique 8

  • Peter Ly's Dallas lab tagged DNA in neighboring cells and filmed fragments traveling through thin membrane connections.
  • Transfer was more common when chromosome segregation was forced to go wrong; some transferred genes stayed active in the recipient cell.
  • Tumor cells appear especially prone to leaving loose DNA outside the nucleus.

Need to know: Lab-dish observation so far. The post notes it has not been shown inside a tumor in vivo.
