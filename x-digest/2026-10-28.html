Glioblastoma

A Mass General CAR-T glioblastoma result is circulating again, with the caveat that most responses were short-lived

For you 7 | Unique 5

  • A widely shared post says a brain tumor 'nearly vanished in just 5 days after one dose' in a Mass General Cancer Center trial and calls it possibly 'the beginning of a cure.'
  • It appears to describe Mass General's early CAR-T work in recurrent glioblastoma, first reported in 2024.
  • A reply under the post notes it was a phase 1 trial in which the rapid regressions lasted only weeks in two of three patients, with one response holding past 150 days.
  • The reply also says solid tumors like glioblastoma remain much harder for CAR-T than blood cancers.

Need to know: This is not new news, and the headline oversells it. It is still a real signal that engineered T cells can reach GBM, and durability is the open problem.

ESMO 2026: five readouts that cut the risk of death the most, plus a personalized mRNA vaccine phase 3

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Divarasib cut the risk of death by 44% versus sotorasib or adagrasib in previously treated KRAS G12C lung cancer (median survival about 18 vs 12 months; phase 3, 338 patients).
  • Durvalumab plus enfortumab vedotin around bladder removal cut the risk of death by 42% in cisplatin-ineligible muscle-invasive bladder cancer (78% vs 69% alive at two years).

Need to know: ESMO's theme is targeted pills and drug-carrying antibodies beating older options. Personalized mRNA vaccines reaching phase 3 matters for the whole cancer-vaccine field, including brain tumors.

Medical AI reality check: chatbots scored well on lung cancer cases, and placement decides whether AI helps

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • A Current Oncology study had four chatbots recommend treatment for 27 new lung cancer cases, and blinded reviewers scored them 4.4 to 4.5 out of 5; Roupen Odabashian notes it was one hospital, a tiny sample and makes no equivalence claim.
  • A JAMA study of 952,000 US clinicians found treatment questions outnumbered diagnosis questions 33% to 14% on an AI reference tool, and oncology and hematology used it at twice the workforce rate (usage only, and some authors are from the vendor).
  • Martin Varsavsky contrasted two October trials: AI suggestions in a hospital record sidebar didn't change anesthesia practice, but AI guiding the needle in CT-guided lung biopsies cut collapsed lungs from 23.9% to 12.7%.

Need to know: AI that does part of the work helped patients. AI that only offers suggestions didn't. That's useful when judging new cancer-AI claims.

Keto updates

Two-year low-carb trial: LDL rose about 13% but ApoB didn't change, and triglycerides and remnant cholesterol fell

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • The CARBFUNC trial, published in eBioMedicine, randomized people with obesity to a low-carb, high-fat diet rich in saturated fat or to high-carb diets for 24 months.
  • Calculated LDL rose by up to about 13% on low carb, but ApoB, the count of atherogenic particles, didn't change significantly at any point.
  • Large, buoyant LDL particles rose while small, dense LDL did not; triglycerides fell 20%, remnant cholesterol fell 23%, and HDL rose throughout.
  • Brian Scherr notes adherence dropped over the two years, a common problem in long diet trials.

Need to know: For anyone on keto, LDL alone can be misleading. ApoB, triglycerides, HDL and remnant cholesterol give a much fuller picture.

Cutting carbs raised calorie burn by about 209 a day, and more in people who secrete the most insulin

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • After weight loss, adults were randomly assigned to 20%, 40% or 60% carbohydrate diets for 20 weeks with protein held constant.
  • Total energy expenditure, measured by doubly labeled water, was about 209 calories a day higher on low carb than high carb.
  • In people with the highest insulin secretion at the start, the gap was about 308 calories a day, and the hunger hormone ghrelin was lower on low carb.
  • In a separate 10-year study of 775 people, resting metabolic rate didn't predict weight gain, but burning mostly glucose after an overnight fast did: lean people who did were 2.4 times as likely to gain 11+ pounds.

Need to know: This supports low carb for keeping weight stable after a loss, not just for losing it.

Vinegar before carbs improved insulin sensitivity by about a third, and Ken Berry's caveat

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • In people with insulin resistance, about 20 g of apple cider vinegar in water before a bagel and orange juice raised insulin sensitivity over the next hour.
  • Ken Berry says it raised insulin sensitivity by about a third, but that doesn't make the bagel and juice a good meal.
  • Bikman adds that a 2017 meta-analysis found vinegar lowered both glucose and insulin responses after meals, and that vinegar with the meal also worked in some studies.

Need to know: Vinegar is a small helper for an occasional carb-heavy meal. It doesn't replace keeping carbs low.

Protein while cutting calories: doubling protein gained lean mass and lost more fat

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • Young men eating about 40% fewer calories than they needed for four weeks while training hard were split into higher and lower protein groups.
  • The group eating twice as much protein gained lean mass and lost more fat.
  • Bikman says the body breaks down more muscle protein when calories are cut, and dietary protein offsets that.

Need to know: This mostly applies during a calorie deficit. When maintaining weight on keto, protein needs are balanced against staying in ketosis.
