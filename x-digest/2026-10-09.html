Cancer updates

Glioblastoma / neuro-oncology

SurVaxM GBM vaccine Phase 2b missed its primary endpoint, with a signal in younger patients

For you 9 | Unique 8

  • MimiVax and Roswell Park's randomized Phase 2b (SURVIVE) of SurVaxM plus standard chemo in newly diagnosed GBM missed its primary overall-survival endpoint.
  • Median OS was 22.8 vs 20.3 months in 233 evaluable patients.
  • In patients 65 and under, 42% were alive at 30 months vs 18% on placebo (subgroup; via @RazorSharpDNA). The company plans FDA talks next.
  • The placebo arm ran more than 4 months ahead of the protocol's assumption (@LifeScienceDN), which made a win harder to show.
  • Avi Roy's thread is a good reminder to read 'shows promise' headlines carefully.

Need to know: It's the biggest GBM trial readout this week. It counts as a miss, with a subgroup signal that would need a confirmatory trial. Research interest only.

Other cancer

Topol: about 15% of ~40,000 cancer patients carry pathogenic germline variants

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • About 15% of roughly 40,000 sequenced cancer patients had disease-causing inherited variants (AACR / Cancer Discovery).
  • Topol argues most of these are missed today, even though they could drive prevention or very early detection.
  • It supports routine germline testing in oncology.

Need to know: Inherited risk is much more common than usually assumed, which matters for families' screening decisions.

For you 8 | Unique 8
