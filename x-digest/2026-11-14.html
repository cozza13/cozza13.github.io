1. Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

UCLA: dendritic-cell vaccine first, then PD-1 blockade, in recurrent GBM (SNO talk today)

For you 9 | Unique 8

  • UCLA's Robert Prins presents CTIM-19 at SNO today (Sat Nov 14), 3:45–3:52 PM ET: PD-1 blockade given after dendritic-cell vaccination versus before.
  • Per a summary from @andrewcaravello, the same three agents (ATL-DC, the DCVax-L-style vaccine, plus poly-ICLC, then Keytruda) gave a median survival of 555 days when the vaccine went first versus 271 days when Keytruda went first.
  • The idea: prime the immune system first to turn a cold tumor hot, then remove the brake.
  • Both posting accounts are $NWBO investors; small, early-phase numbers, so check the abstract.

Need to know: Sequencing matters is the takeaway. These are small numbers from investor accounts, so wait for the talk or abstract before reading much into the exact survival figures.

For you 9 | Unique 8

UCSF brain-restricted CAR-T wipes out diffuse midline glioma in mice

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • UCSF engineered CAR-T cells with a "brain-only" switch: a sensor for brevican, a protein found only in brain tissue.
  • The cells stay inactive in blood and spleen and only arm themselves against tumor proteins once in the brain.
  • Cleared diffuse midline glioma (a pediatric glioma) in mice. Preclinical only.

Need to know: A neat safety design that could carry over to other brain tumors, but it is mouse data.

Other cancer

FDA approves durvalumab + enfortumab vedotin around surgery for bladder cancer (VOLGA)

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • FDA approved durvalumab + EV before and after cystectomy for cisplatin-ineligible muscle-invasive bladder cancer on Nov 13.
  • VOLGA: 2-year event-free survival 64% vs 45% (HR 0.51); 2-year overall survival 78% vs 69%.
  • Second EV-based perioperative regimen now approved, alongside EV + pembrolizumab.

Need to know: Continues the trend of antibody-drug conjugates plus immunotherapy moving earlier in treatment.
