Logic-gated personalized T-cell therapies are being made from tumor workups

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • "An increasing number of our patients are using Valius workup results to manufacture logic-gated T-cells with academic and commercial groups across the country."
  • "Logic gates theoretically hit a bunch of the biggest problems: tumor specificity, antigen redundancy, etc."
  • "Much work to be done over time (persistence, trafficking, etc), but this feels like a modality that can be hillclimbed."

Need to know: Logic-gated CAR/T cells only fire when a tumor shows a combination of antigens. That approach matters for solid tumors like GBM, where antigens vary across the tumor. It's an early modality, and persistence and trafficking are still open problems.

FDA approves adjuvant atezolizumab for stage III dMMR colon cancer (ATOMIC)

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Atezolizumab + fluoropyrimidine/oxaliplatin after resection, stage III dMMR colon cancer.
  • ATOMIC: 712 randomised 1:1, atezo + mFOLFOX6 then 6 mo atezo vs mFOLFOX6.
  • DFS HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.35-0.73), p=0.0001; median DFS not reached in either arm.
  • No OS data in the FDA notice; subcutaneous Tecentriq Hybreza also approved.

Need to know: This is another approval that puts checkpoint blockade into earlier lines for mismatch-repair-deficient tumors. @dr_dirican's weekly roundup also counts HER2CLIMB-05 and EPCORE DLBCL-2 as wins, and FURVENT as having a high response rate but a negative primary endpoint.
