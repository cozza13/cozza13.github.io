3. Keto updates

Seyfried: restrict glucose and glutamine while ketones are elevated

For you 8 | Unique 6

  • Thomas Seyfried argued that cancers make lactate from glucose and succinate from glutamine, marking those two as the metabolic drivers.
  • He proposed restricting both while ketones are raised through nutritional ketosis, possibly combined with low-dose chemo and immunotherapy.

Need to know: This is his long-standing metabolic hypothesis restated, not new trial data, but it is exactly the ketosis lever you focus on.
