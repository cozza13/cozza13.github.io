OpenAI's startup fund and Founders Fund back Coherence Neuro's $45M seed for a GBM-sensing brain implant

For you 8 | Unique 8

GBM research roundup: CellShape-seq stem-cell states, tumor treating fields before radiotherapy, and UPR review

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Human Technopole's CellShape-seq (in Neuro-Oncology) links cell shape to gene activity to pick out distinct GBM stem-like cell states and treatment responses.
  • BMC Cancer published the PriCoTTF phase I/II design testing tumor treating fields started before and during radiotherapy in newly diagnosed GBM.
  • A Journal of Cell Science review covers the unfolded protein response's roles in glioblastoma.
  • A prospective pilot (FETSMaRT) found volume differences between FET-PET/MRI and MRI-based radiotherapy targets in high-grade glioma.

Need to know: Mostly methods and early-trial items rather than outcomes. The TTFields-timing trial and the FET-PET target work are the most practical for following how standard care is evolving.

Keto updates

Bikman: same glucose response after two meals, but insulin was much higher after the ultra-processed one

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Ben Bikman highlights a study where total glucose matched after a whole-food meal and an ultra-processed meal.
  • Insulin was significantly higher after the ultra-processed meal.
  • His point is that a glucose monitor alone would have rated the meals equal.
  • He also notes that enlarged fat cells predicted type 2 diabetes better than body-fat percentage in a long-term study.

Need to know: A reminder that glucose and ketone readings don't capture insulin; fasting insulin is one of the labs already in your tracker history.

@BenBikmanPhD on X

Keto cut liver fat 67% versus 45% on Mediterranean or plant-forward diets, but protein was higher

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • A Cell Metabolism trial with all food provided and weight loss matched found keto reduced liver fat by 67%, versus 45% for the comparison diets.
  • The keto arm was also much higher in protein (about 23% of energy versus about 15%).
  • Nicola Guess argues the protein difference, not ketones, may explain the result; commenters call for a protein-matched keto arm.

Need to know: Relevant if you're thinking about how high protein can go on a 3:1 ratio, since the liver benefit may come from carbs being down and protein being up, not ketosis itself.
