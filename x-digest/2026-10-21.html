Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Duke phase 1 (Nature Medicine): EGFR/EGFRvIII antibody-toxin pumped straight into recurrent GBM

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • The drug, D2C7 ATC, is an antibody-toxin that recognizes both EGFR and its mutant form EGFRvIII.
  • Instead of relying on it to cross the blood-brain barrier, the team infused it directly into the tumor by convection-enhanced delivery.
  • 81 patients enrolled; 72 had recurrent IDH-wildtype glioblastoma, whose median overall survival was 7.9 months.
  • About two-thirds of adverse events were mild, per @rababcancerfou1's summary.
  • Some exploratory biomarker groups (EGFR expression, IL-8) did better, but it was single-arm, so benefit is unproven.

Need to know: A direct-delivery approach aimed at the most common GBM driver. The biomarker angle (who responds, by EGFR level) is the part worth following.

Keto updates

Cell Metabolism trial: keto cut liver fat 67% vs 45%, but protein may explain part of it

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • In a feeding trial with all food provided and weight loss matched, a ketogenic diet reduced liver fat by 67%, versus 45% on Mediterranean or plant-forward diets.
  • The keto arm was also much higher in protein (23% of energy vs about 15%).
  • Nutrition scientist Nicola Guess argues the protein, not the ketones, did much of the work.
  • Commenters note a protein-matched keto arm would settle it (@med_heads).

Need to know: A strong result for low-carb on fatty liver with a real confounder. Worth noting for anyone weighing how much protein a ketogenic plan should include.

@theproof on X

Bikman: same glucose, more insulin after ultra-processed food; fat-cell size predicts diabetes

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • In a study of matched meals, total glucose was the same after both, but insulin was significantly higher after the ultra-processed one (also shared by @foundmyfitness).
  • Bikman's point is that a glucose monitor would have called the meals equal; insulin showed they weren't.
  • Separately: enlarged fat cells, not body-fat percentage, predicted who developed type 2 diabetes in a long-term study (Weyer et al., Diabetologia 2000).
  • 10 days of bed rest cut muscle protein synthesis about 30% in healthy older adults and took nearly 1 kg of lean tissue off the legs.

Need to know: Another argument for watching fasting insulin, not just glucose. @longevitymediaa also summarized Jason Fung saying insulin rises years before blood sugar.
