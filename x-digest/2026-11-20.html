UK review: neuro-oncology patients at Coventry (UHCW) kept on temozolomide far beyond standard

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • University Hospitals Coventry & Warwickshire published a Royal College of Physicians invited review of its neuro-oncology service on Nov 18.
  • Reviewers looked at 20 patients given temozolomide for more than 12 cycles (2017-2023): 15 cases were rated unsatisfactory and 5 needed improvement, with some patients potentially severely harmed.
  • The review cites governance and oversight failures and makes 13 recommendations; the trust accepts them and is arranging independent reviews of living patients who had extended treatment. BBC reports hundreds could be affected.
  • Local MP Mary Creagh posted a statement; this is the same story the keto-skeptic community was discussing yesterday.

Need to know: A governance story, not a new-science story: it is about how long one drug was continued and who checked. Useful context for asking a care team how long a regimen is meant to run and what would end it.

UHCW statement · BBC · Mary Creagh

Alpha Tau REGAIN (Alpha DaRT, recurrent GBM): 8 of 8 alive in an early US trial; FDA allows expansion to 20

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • Alpha Tau's Q3 2026 update (Nov 19): 8 patients treated with Alpha DaRT (radium-224 seeds placed in the tumor that release alpha particles), all alive as of Nov 11; median overall survival not reached.
  • Among 6 evaluable patients, objective response 100% with 5 complete responses; deployment was feasible in all 8 with no unanticipated serious adverse events (company-reported).
  • FDA approved expanding the trial from 10 to up to 20 patients and adding progression-free survival and quality-of-life endpoints; more interim data expected Q1 2027.
  • On X this is being pushed mostly by $DRTS stock accounts. It is a company update on 8 people, not a peer-reviewed paper.

Need to know: Interesting early signal for a local radiation approach in recurrent GBM, but very small, short follow-up and company-reported. Watch for the Q1 2027 data and a publication.

Alpha Tau release · SNO interim results

UCSF Chang Lab in Science: a patch of Wernicke's area plans each word before you say it

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • UCSF neurosurgery chair Eddie Chang's lab reports in Science that a subregion of Wernicke's area (left temporal lobe, long described as "listening only") activates ~0.25 s before speaking and is needed for speech production.
  • Andrew Huberman amplified it; Chang is now on X as @EddieChangMD.
  • Relevant to awake brain-mapping surgery and speech-sparing approaches to tumors near language areas.

Need to know: Basic neuroscience from a UCSF team, not a tumor study, but it shapes how surgeons map language before resections.

3. Keto updates

11.8 million paired glucose-ketone readings: "Morning trough, evening peak"

For you 9 | Unique 8

  • New Nutrition & Metabolism paper (Soto-Mota, Spritzler et al.) analyzed paired blood BHB and glucose readings from 138,330 Keto-Mojo users.
  • Within individuals, each 1 mmol/L rise in BHB went with roughly 6 mg/dL lower glucose.
  • Ketones tended to be lowest in the morning and peak in the evening.
  • Observational, self-selected meter users; not a trial.

Need to know: Directly relevant to anyone tracking GKI: time of day matters for ketone readings, so compare like with like.

Keto-Mojo · Paper

Bikman: in mild Alzheimer's, brain glucose uptake falls but ketone uptake stays normal

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • PET imaging studies show lower gray-matter glucose use in mild Alzheimer's, most in typically affected regions, while ketone uptake matches healthy older adults.
  • Bikman argues ketones remain a usable brain fuel when glucose use falls (6,400+ views, study linked in thread).

Need to know: Brain-energy evidence for ketones in neurodegeneration, not tumors; good background on brain fuel use.

Virta: telehealth carb-reduced nutrition linked to 56% lower risk of heart attack, stroke or death

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Retrospective claims study in Cardiology and Therapy: 4,877 adults with type 2 diabetes or obesity in Virta's program vs propensity-matched usual care; HR 0.44 for heart attack, stroke or death.

Need to know: Adds to the cardiovascular-safety picture for ketogenic nutrition, which keeps coming up in LDL debates.
