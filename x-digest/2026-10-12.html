Glioblastoma / neuro-oncology

NRG-BN010: tocilizumab + atezolizumab + FSRT safe but misses ORR endpoint in recurrent GBM

For you 9 | Unique 9

  • Phase 2 primary analysis now in Red Journal (articles in press; PubMed 42810412): tocilizumab (anti-IL-6R) + atezolizumab (anti-PD-L1) + fractionated stereotactic RT (24 Gy in 3 fractions).
  • ORR by mRANO was 3.4% (1/29) in the phase 2 non-surgical cohort — did not meet the pre-specified efficacy bar (null ≤10% vs alt ≥30%).
  • Median PFS 3.2 months; median OS 10.2 months. Safety run-in set MTD as tocilizumab 8 mg/kg + atezolizumab 1680 mg with FSRT; regimen was "safe and well tolerated."
  • Authors: combination feasible for high-dose-per-fraction RT with IO in GBM, but "alternative approaches are needed" for antitumor immunity.

Need to know: This is a real cooperative-group negative for IL-6R + PD-L1 + FSRT in first-recurrence GBM. Interesting for the IL-6/macrophage biology story, and a reminder that checkpoint + stereotactic combos still struggle in cold GBM microenvironments. Surgical window-of-opportunity correlatives are still pending. Research interest only — not treatment advice.

Other cancer

Universal germline testing: age cutoffs would miss most hereditary cancer variant carriers

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Cancer Discovery study of 39,184 patients across 32 solid tumor types: 16.3% carried a pathogenic germline variant.
  • Testing only patients diagnosed before 50 "would miss 72% of carriers"; 12.3% of late-onset patients still carried one.
  • @Aiims1742 flagged a comparable NYU/Natera study of >153,000 people: strict criteria "may miss 22%" of actionable carriers.

Need to know: Two large datasets push toward broader germline testing rather than age/family-history gates. Open question: how often a found variant actually changes treatment.

Bikman: the 20–30 g protein-per-meal limit isn't real; 100 g kept muscle synthesis going 12+ hours

For you 8 | Unique 6

  • Old cap "mostly came from studies where muscle protein synthesis leveled off after a modest dose of whey after lifting."
  • With 100 g after exercise, "muscles kept making new protein for more than 12 hours", more than with 25 g, and "very little of the extra protein was burned off as fuel."
  • "Stop counting grams per meal and focus on the daily total."

Need to know: On a 3:1 ratio, protein is tightly budgeted. This framing says meal splitting matters less than the daily total when planning a keto day. Research interest — not a prescription.
