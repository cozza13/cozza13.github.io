Keto updates

Ben Bikman study: brain tissue kept running on the ketone BHB alone, with no glucose

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Slices of mouse brain were kept in fluid with no glucose and only beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) as fuel.
  • After 4 hours the mitochondria were still producing energy, and the tissue could repeatedly handle energy-intensive electrical waves; tissue with no fuel couldn't.
  • Bikman calls it the first evidence suggesting the brain may not strictly require glucose.
  • In a second post he highlighted a study finding liver fat, not pancreatic fat, tracked insulin resistance in people with diabetes (association only).

Need to know: Interesting basic science on brain fuel use. It's mouse tissue in a dish, so it says nothing directly about people or tumors, but it's relevant background for metabolic therapy research.
