Glioblastoma

Russia files to register "Gliorna," a personalized mRNA glioblastoma vaccine

For you 7 | Unique 8

  • FMBA deputy head Igor Borisevich said Nov 18 that the registration dossier for an mRNA vaccine for glioblastoma is under expert review at Russia's Health Ministry (RIA Novosti, Gazeta).
  • Russian outlets name it Gliorna: personalized to each patient's tumor. FMBA says preclinical studies are complete; a peptide vaccine, Gliopept, is on a parallel track.
  • No human efficacy or safety data has been published that we could find. Filing for registration is not approval.

Need to know: Headline-grabbing, but there is no peer-reviewed clinical data behind it yet. Worth watching for any published trial results; treat claims with caution until then.

For you 6 | Unique 6

Other cancer

Phase 3: a WT1 vaccine (galinpepimut-S) extended survival in AML maintenance

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • Sellas tested galinpepimut-S in 126 adults with AML in second or later remission who were not transplant candidates.
  • Posts cite median OS of about 24 vs 14.7 months, roughly a 41% lower risk of death.
  • A randomized Phase 3 survival benefit for a cancer vaccine is rare.

Need to know: Not brain cancer, but a randomized win for a peptide vaccine in residual disease is a useful reference point for vaccine strategies in general.

3. Keto updates

Bikman: high glucose glycates insulin itself, making it weaker

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • In type 2 diabetes, glycated insulin makes up close to a tenth of circulating insulin.
  • When infused into healthy men at everyday levels, it took substantially more glycated insulin to clear the same glucose.
  • Proposed loop: higher glucose leads to weaker insulin, which leads to more insulin secretion. His book The Carb Crisis is out Dec 15.

Need to know: One more mechanism tying sustained low glucose to better insulin signaling. Mechanistic and human infusion data.
