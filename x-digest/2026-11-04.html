UCSF engineered CAR-T cells that switch on only inside the brain cleared diffuse midline glioma in mice

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • UCSF scientists engineered CAR-T cells designed to activate only inside the brain.
  • Reported to have wiped out diffuse midline glioma tumors in every mouse tested.
  • Mice also showed lasting protection afterward.

Need to know: This is mouse work on a related high-grade glioma, not GBM itself. Keto updates

Ben Bikman: inactivity, high insulin itself, and sub-diabetic glucose all tied to worse metabolic outcomes

For you 8 | Unique 6

  • Muscle is the largest glucose consumer; within days of inactivity even lean healthy people become measurably insulin resistant, and single-leg cast studies show the effect is local.
  • Healthy young volunteers given steady insulin infusion for about four days became insulin resistant despite normal glucose, so high insulin can feed itself.
  • In older adults, higher five-year average glucose predicted dementia even below the diabetes range; he flags this as observational.
  • He ties daily carbohydrate intake to average glucose.

Need to know: Fits your levers of low insulin and low glucose, and the muscle point adds a movement angle for keeping GKI low. These are his summaries of studies; the dementia link is observational and does not prove cause.

Insulin resistance as an early warning: Finnish 20-year cohort and Whitehall II

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Finnish Health 2000 cohort (Neurobiology of Aging): insulin resistance around age 55 was linked to faster memory decline about two decades later.
  • Reported: memory declined about twice as fast over 5 years of late-life follow-up in the higher insulin-resistance group.
  • Whitehall II: tracked 505 people who developed type 2 diabetes back up to 13 years; fasting glucose and A1C stay normal for years while insulin resistance builds.

Need to know: Both support looking at insulin-related markers, not only glucose or A1C. These are posts summarising papers, so the exact effect sizes are worth checking in the sources.
