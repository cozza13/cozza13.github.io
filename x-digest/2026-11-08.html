CAR-T for glioblastoma: two clinical case series recapped, plus a multi-antigen mouse study

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • @exosome recapped CAR-T cells built to secrete an EGFR-targeting T-cell engager; in three patients tumors regressed within days, though responses were transient in two.
  • A second recap: in one GBM patient, IL13Rα2 CAR-T delivered into the brain's ventricles cleared all detectable brain and spinal tumors for 7.5 months before recurrence.
  • A third post covers synNotch CAR-T cells that recognize multiple GBM antigens, improving killing and persistence in mice.
  • These are summaries of earlier published work, not new results.

Need to know: A useful refresher on why GBM CAR-T keeps hitting the same walls (antigen escape and delivery) and the engineering fixes being tried.

Bikman: two short nights drop leptin, raise ghrelin, and drive carb cravings

For you 8 | Unique 6

  • In a controlled study, healthy young men slept less for two days with food and activity held steady.
  • Leptin fell, ghrelin rose, and appetite climbed most for calorie-dense, high-carb foods.
  • Bikman's takeaway: sleep regulates next-day hunger.

Need to know: Lines up with your sleep-plus-ketosis focus: short sleep makes staying under 20 g net carbs harder.

Bikman: high fasting insulin tied to Alzheimer's even after adjusting for APOE4; gut microbes improved insulin sensitivity in a small RCT

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • A Finnish cross-sectional study linked high fasting insulin with Alzheimer's after adjusting for APOE4, age, blood pressure, cholesterol and glucose.
  • It can't show cause and effect.
  • A separate small randomized trial of lean-donor gut microbe transfer improved insulin sensitivity in men with metabolic syndrome, measured by glucose clamp.

Need to know: More support for tracking fasting insulin, not just glucose, as a metabolic marker.
