Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Loperamide (the drug in Imodium) pushes glioblastoma cells into self-destruction in the lab

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Researchers at Goethe University Frankfurt report that loperamide triggers cell death in lab-grown glioblastoma cells.
  • The proposed mechanism is severe endoplasmic reticulum stress, which activates the ATF4 protein and a recycling process called reticulophagy.
  • The post stresses that this is lab work only, not a human trial, and that high-dose loperamide has dangerous heart side effects.
  • Separately, ResearchHub drafted a small study to test whether loperamide kills patient-derived GBM organoids at lower doses than matched healthy neurons and astrocytes.

Need to know: This is an early drug-repurposing signal worth following for the mechanism, not something to act on. The next useful step is the organoid selectivity test, which would show whether tumor cells die at doses that spare normal brain cells.

Glioblastoma organoids grown on the ISS for 40 days came back more aggressive-looking

For you 6 | Unique 8

  • A PNAS study grew GBM organoids in microgravity on the International Space Station for 40 days.
  • They returned more compact than ground controls.
  • Adding monocytes (immune cells) made them look more like aggressive human GBM, with tumor-like core and edge gene patterns.
  • The authors frame microgravity as a new way to model tumors, not just a hazard.

Need to know: A niche but genuinely new research model. It matters mainly if it gives labs a better stand-in for how real GBM tumors organize their core and invasive edge.

Other oncology

Final CheckMate 8HW results: dual checkpoint blockade holds a survival edge in MSI-H colorectal cancer

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • With 68 months of follow-up, nivolumab plus ipilimumab beat nivolumab alone on overall survival across all lines (HR 0.61).
  • In first line it beat chemotherapy on overall survival (HR 0.37).
  • Progression-free survival and response advantages held up, with no new safety signals.

Need to know: It's the strongest long-term evidence yet that combining two checkpoint drugs pays off in this biomarker-selected colorectal group. It's mostly a reference point for how immunotherapy combos are maturing.

Keto updates

Ben Bikman: the same dinner eaten later does more metabolic harm, and A1C can mislead

For you 7 | Unique 5

  • Bikman cites a study where healthy young adults ate the identical dinner once at 6 PM and once later, and compared how their bodies handled it.
  • His point is that energy balance is real, but meal timing changes how the body handles the same calories.
  • In a separate post he explained that a high A1C doesn't always mean high blood sugar, because A1C measures glucose stuck to hemoglobin in red blood cells rather than blood sugar directly.

Need to know: This lines up with your focus on sleep and an earlier last meal. If your A1C ever looks out of step with your meter or CGM numbers, his A1C post explains why that can happen.
