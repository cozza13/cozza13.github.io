Glioblastoma

New paper: the enzyme PHGDH helps IDH-mutant glioma resist IDH inhibitors, and it shows up on metabolic imaging

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • An AACR journal account posted a newly online paper titled 'PHGDH Sustains Oncometabolite Production to Drive Resistance to Mutant IDH Inhibition in Glioma.'
  • PHGDH is an enzyme in the serine pathway; the paper links it to keeping the tumor's oncometabolite production going despite mutant-IDH inhibitor drugs.
  • The title says this resistance can be monitored with metabolic imaging.
  • The post itself only gives the title and a link, so the details are in the paper.

Need to know: This is a metabolic escape route from IDH-inhibitor drugs, with a possible imaging readout, which makes it a good fit for the metabolic view of brain tumors. It applies to IDH-mutant gliomas, not IDH-wildtype GBM, and is for research reading only.

Other oncology

ESMO 2026: daraxonrasib roughly doubles survival in previously treated pancreatic cancer (RASolute 302)

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • The phase III RASolute 302 trial (500 patients) compared the RAS(ON) inhibitor daraxonrasib with chemotherapy in previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer.
  • Median overall survival was 13.2 vs 6.7 months (HR 0.40), PFS HR was 0.55, and the response rate was 32% vs 11%.
  • The survival benefit held for the G12D (HR 0.49) and G12V (HR 0.32) mutations, with a weaker signal for G12R (HR 0.61).
  • Separately, Tango reported an 82% response rate for daraxonrasib plus its drug vopimetostat, using a lower daraxonrasib dose (via @Aiims1742).

Need to know: This is one of the strongest results yet for drugs that block RAS broadly, and posters are calling it a new second-line standard. The data are from pancreatic cancer only.

Moderna and Merck's personalized mRNA melanoma vaccine: data out early after a leak, and the stock fell anyway

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • The phase III INTerpath-001 abstract leaked before the embargo, so ESMO released it early.
  • Adding intismeran (mRNA-4157) to pembrolizumab after surgery for stage IIB-IV melanoma gave 24-month recurrence-free survival of 82.2% vs 70.0% (HR 0.58).
  • 24-month distant-metastasis-free survival was 88.5% vs 80.2% (HR 0.62); serious (grade 3 or higher) side effects were 23.6% vs 20.3%.
  • Moderna fell as much as 23% (@Polymarket); Citi kept a Sell rating, noting the SWOG S1801 trial reached a similar HR of 0.58 just by giving pembrolizumab before surgery (@venaodiver).

Need to know: This is the first large phase III win for a vaccine built from each patient's own tumor mutations. The live debate is how much it adds compared with simply giving immunotherapy earlier. There is still no FDA approval.

Breast cancer and FDA: NATALEE six-year survival data, and Keytruda's TMB-high approval made full with a stricter cutoff

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • At six years, adding ribociclib after surgery in HR+/HER2- early breast cancer showed overall survival of 91.9% vs 89.7% (HR 0.81).
  • The absolute survival gain was 1.2% at 5 years and 2.1% at 6 years, and the invasive-disease-free survival benefit held up.
  • Some oncologists say it's 'not ready to call NATALEE an OS win' and describe it as clinically meaningful but not confirmatory (@KolPulseAI).
  • Separately, the FDA converted pembrolizumab's tumor-agnostic TMB-high approval to full approval and raised the cutoff from 10 or more to 13 or more mutations per megabase (@ozdogan_md).

Need to know: Small but lasting survival gains get argued over a lot at ESMO. The TMB change matters for anyone who reads tumor-mutation-burden numbers on a genomic report, because the threshold for that approval is now higher.

AI updates

Black October, day five: LiteLLM's poisoned release stole AI keys

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • LiteLLM, a widely used library for calling OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini (about 23 million downloads a week), shipped a poisoned version 1.107.0 on October 21 on PyPI and Docker.
  • Posters say it hid a credential stealer that runs whenever Python starts and went after AI API keys, Claude, Codex, Gemini and Cursor auth files, cloud credentials, SSH keys and browser passwords.
  • It was on PyPI for about 1 hour 44 minutes (around 49,000 downloads); the advice is to pin 1.106.2, delete the leftover .pth files (uninstalling doesn't remove them) and rotate every key from a clean machine (@mardehaym).
  • A general checklist making the rounds: patch PHP and Node, update browsers and phones, wait 24 hours before installing brand-new pip/npm releases, and cap and rotate API keys (@altryne).

Need to know: This is the new development since yesterday's PHP bug story: the attacks are now going after AI developer tools and keys. If LiteLLM sits anywhere in a pipeline, checking the installed version is the first step.

Keto updates

Bikman: at the same weight loss, very-low-carb improved liver insulin sensitivity 2 to 3 times more

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Ben Bikman highlighted a trial where weight loss was matched at about 10% across diets.
  • He says the very-low-carb group improved liver insulin sensitivity two to three times more than the Mediterranean or very-low-fat groups, and also did better on 24-hour insulin levels.
  • In a separate post he notes glucose is 'the last marker to move': in the Whitehall II study, insulin sensitivity was already falling sharply about 5 years before a diabetes diagnosis, while glucose rose steeply only in the last 3.
  • He suggests fasting insulin with fasting glucose (HOMA-IR) as an earlier signal than glucose alone.

Need to know: This supports the idea that carb restriction helps metabolism beyond weight loss, and that insulin tells you more than glucose alone. Personal-interest reading only.

Bikman on coffee and insulin, and on why retatrutide eased sleep apnea

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Bikman says morning coffee 'isn't metabolically neutral': in a 1978 study, 250 mg of caffeine raised epinephrine 207% in people who didn't usually drink coffee.
  • In a 2002 study, IV caffeine lowered insulin sensitivity by 15% in healthy adults; he adds that habitual drinkers adapt somewhat and decaf doesn't seem to act the same way.
  • He also notes Lilly's retatrutide cut sleep apnea events by up to 24 per hour versus placebo in two NEJM trials, and says he thinks that comes from the 12 to 22% weight loss rather than the drug treating the airway.
  • On GLP-1 drugs, he stresses eating protein first and says about two-thirds of the weight came back within a year of stopping semaglutide in STEP 1.

Need to know: Caffeine can nudge morning glucose readings, which is worth knowing when interpreting them. The retatrutide point is his interpretation of the trials, not the authors'.
