For you 7 | Unique 7

  • Phase 3 SWOG/NRG S1914 tested induction plus consolidation atezolizumab with SBRT in high-risk early-stage NSCLC.

Need to know: A clean negative phase 3 result for combining immunotherapy with radiation in early-stage disease.
