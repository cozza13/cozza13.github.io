Neuro-onc journal notes: FET PET and seizures in IDH-mutant glioma; brain effects of immunotherapy

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • J Neuro-Oncol: in a retrospective study of 121 IDH-mutant lower-grade glioma patients, higher FET PET uptake was linked to higher odds of seizures before surgery (adjusted OR 2.86).
  • Cancer (journal): survivors of advanced cancer treated with immune checkpoint blockade scored worse than matched controls on neurocognitive tests, especially learning and long-term memory, but improved gradually over time.
  • Both are observational studies, tagged #neuroonc.

Need to know: Two small observational signals: FET PET as more than a tumor-extent tool, and a reminder that immunotherapy can carry measurable cognitive effects that may recover.

Other cancer

RAS pill daraxonrasib + chemo in first-line metastatic pancreatic cancer: 61% response, 12.8-month PFS

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Presented at ENA 2026 in Barcelona: daraxonrasib (oral RAS(ON) inhibitor, Revolution Medicines, brand Rasonque) plus gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel in 61 untreated patients.
  • Response rate ~61% (8% complete), median progression-free survival 12.8 months, ~77% alive at one year; historical chemo alone (MPACT) was ~23% response and 5.5-month PFS.
  • Small and non-randomized; the phase 3 RASolute-303 trial is the real test. The separate randomized second-line trial (~500 people) showed 13.2 vs 6.7 months median survival and underpins the FDA approval in August.
  • Reported price around $480,000/year.

Need to know: The biggest oncology story on X this week. RAS was long called undruggable; this is the strongest sign yet that pan-RAS inhibition works in a very hard cancer. The headline "90%" is disease control in a single-arm study, not cure.

3. Keto updates

Bikman: muscle insulin resistance pushes meal carbs to the liver as fat, even in lean young people

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Muscle is the main store for post-meal carbohydrate (glycogen); when it resists insulin, more of the carbohydrate goes to the liver and is converted to fat.
  • He notes this happens even in young, lean people, not just obesity.
  • Ben Bikman was also announced as a PHC Conference 2027 speaker.

Need to know: A clean mechanistic case for why muscle (and resistance training) matters for metabolic health independent of body weight.

@BenBikmanPhD

Sleep regularity beats sleep length in a new analysis shared by Bryan Johnson

For you 7 | Unique 5

  • Consistent bed and wake times were linked to better memory, thinking, mood, brain volume and white-matter health.
  • Regularity outperformed total sleep duration on every measure reported (observational data).

Need to know: If you track sleep, bed/wake consistency may be worth logging alongside duration and score.
