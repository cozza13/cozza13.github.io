Paricalcitol plus chemo in metastatic pancreatic cancer: 42% vs ~9% partial response in small trial

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Paricalcitol is "a drug related to vitamin D" and was tested with standard chemo in "36 patients with metastatic pancreatic cancer".
  • "42% of patients receiving paricalcitol and chemotherapy experienced partial tumor shrinkage, compared with about 9%" on chemo plus placebo.
  • Appeared to reduce cancer-associated fibroblast activity, which targets the stromal "shield".

Need to know: It's a pop-science summary of a small trial, so treat it as hypothesis-level. The stroma-remodeling idea (making dense tumors reachable for immune cells and drugs) is a theme that shows up across hard-to-treat solid tumors.

CLDN18.2 status changes after therapy in about a quarter of gastric cancers

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • 155 gastric/GEJ patients with paired biopsies before and after systemic therapy.
  • 21.8% of negatives became positive and 27.8% of positives became negative.
  • No difference between chemo and chemo-IO in conversion (p=0.45); expression shifted in 84.5% overall.
  • "Just like HER2... we will need to re-test CLDN before a later-line CLDN18.2 drug."

Need to know: A general lesson that applies beyond gastric cancer: target biomarkers drift under treatment, so a single baseline test can go stale. GBM targets like EGFR are a similar case.
