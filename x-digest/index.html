Glioblastoma

Trotabresib adds nothing to chemoradiation in newly diagnosed IDH-wildtype GBM

For you 9 | Unique 8

  • CancerNetwork: trotabresib (a BET inhibitor) plus postoperative chemoradiotherapy gave a median PFS of 7.9 months vs 8.2 months with chemoradiotherapy alone.
  • Population: newly diagnosed IDH-wildtype glioblastoma.
  • A negative result: no PFS signal.

Need to know: One more add-on to the standard backbone that did not move progression-free survival. Useful for keeping the SNO positives in proportion.

CancerNetwork

UCSF gene therapy DB107-RRV can be given alongside radiation in newly diagnosed high-grade glioma

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • UCSF-led Phase 1/2 (NCT06504381), interim SNO 2026 poster: a virus vector (formerly Toca 511) injected into the cavity wall at surgery and given by IV, then a prodrug pill during radiation, with or without temozolomide.
  • By the Oct 15 cutoff: 28 evaluable patients, 16 still on treatment, 15 treated for more than 180 days, the longest 618 days. 6 stopped for progression.
  • Main point is feasibility and safety; no survival data yet.

Need to know: A UCSF trial for newly diagnosed patients, so it is mainly something to know exists. Emerging evidence: early, single-arm.
