Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Cell study: cutting a tumor-made metabolite (GAA) calms neurons and slows glioma in mice

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • A Cell paper reports that gliomas release guanidinoacetate (GAA), a creatine precursor, which drives nearby neurons into hyperactivity.
  • Lowering tumor-derived GAA reduced that neuronal hyperactivity and made tumors less aggressive in mice.
  • It fits the cancer-neuroscience idea that gliomas grow by wiring into and feeding off neural activity.
  • Separately, Wistar scientists described a two-pronged strategy to overcome GBM's resistance to immunotherapy (via @newswise).

Need to know: A metabolic lever on the neuron-tumor feedback loop, which is the same biology Coherence Neuro is betting on electrically. Mouse-only so far.

For you 7 | Unique 7

ESMO 2026 opens in Madrid: EU approves EV plus pembro in bladder cancer; ESCAT v2 published

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • The European Commission approved perioperative enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab for resectable muscle-invasive bladder cancer, regardless of cisplatin eligibility.

AI updates

AI-found PHP bugs force patch-or-go-offline warnings; CVEs jump to about 15,000 a month

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • 'PHP2Shell' is two critical PHP bugs, hidden for 14 years, that let attackers run code on web servers without logging in. They were found with OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol through its 'Patch the Planet' program.
  • The NSA, the FBI and Japan's prime minister told sites to patch or go offline; Polymarket reported Wikipedia, Steam and government sites going offline.
  • OpenAI expects such findings to 'increase sharply' and committed $1B in subsidized cyber-model access for defenders.
  • @saranormous: CVEs went from about 4,000 a month to about 15,000 since April, and fixes need to run at machine speed too.

Need to know: The first big real-world case of frontier models finding critical infrastructure bugs at scale. Patch anything you run on PHP.
