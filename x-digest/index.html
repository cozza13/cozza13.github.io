FDA approves atezolizumab with FOLFOX-type chemo as adjuvant therapy for stage III dMMR colon cancer

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Approval covers atezolizumab plus a fluoropyrimidine and oxaliplatin, for adults and children 2 and older; the subcutaneous form (Tecentriq Hybreza) is approved from age 12 and 40 kg.
  • Based on the ATOMIC trial: disease-free survival hazard ratio 0.50.
  • 3-year DFS was about 86.3% vs 76.2%.
  • A Halozyme analyst note followed, since Halozyme's enzyme powers the subcutaneous version.

Need to know: Checkpoint immunotherapy keeps moving earlier, into the post-surgery setting, for tumors with mismatch-repair deficiency. The approval date reported elsewhere was Oct 8; it circulated widely yesterday.

An oncologist pushes back on the idea that sugar "feeds" cancer

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Dr. Loaiza-Bonilla separates "Did sugar cause my cancer?" from "Is sugar feeding my cancer?", which he says patients ask constantly.
  • His view: a tumor "will eat anything that is on its way, glucose or fat or protein."
  • He says patients in treatment need calories, and "there is no established evidence that restricting sugar improves outcomes" for people who already have cancer.

Need to know: This is the mainstream oncology position, and it sits directly against the metabolic-therapy view (Seyfried and others) that tumors depend heavily on glucose and glutamine. It's useful to know how the opposing case is being framed.

ACTION-1: first positive phase 3 for an alpha-emitting radioligand, in neuroendocrine tumors after lutetium

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • 225Ac-DOTATATE (RYZ101) improved PFS versus standard options in SSTR-positive GEP neuroendocrine tumors that progressed after 177Lu therapy.
  • Actinium is an alpha emitter: very short range and much higher energy transfer than lutetium's beta radiation.
  • Open questions he lists: hazard ratio and absolute benefit, kidney and bone-marrow toxicity, overall survival, and isotope supply.
  • @sonbol_bassam called it the "First Phase 3 data for an alpha RPT."

Need to know: Alpha radioligands have been a promise for years, and this is the first phase 3 readout behind them. Full data, especially long-term toxicity, will decide how big it is.

Keto updates

Bikman: insulin is the switch on fat burning, and Alzheimer's brains still take up ketones

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Brain tissue from people with Alzheimer's, even without diabetes, "responds poorly to insulin," and it worsens from healthy to MCI to Alzheimer's.
  • On PET, the Alzheimer's brain "still takes up ketones normally even when its glucose use is down by about 11%."
  • In a separate post: even a small insulin rise "sharply cuts the release of fat from fat cells" and the ketone supply with it.

Need to know: A clean summary of the brain-energy argument for ketones as an alternative fuel, the same logic that underlies metabolic therapy research.
