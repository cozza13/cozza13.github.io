Glioblastoma

Mouse study: blocking immune-tumor signals made glioblastoma less invasive without shrinking it

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • Researchers blocked signaling between immune cells and glioblastoma cells in mice.
  • The tumors didn't get smaller, but they spread less into surrounding brain tissue.
  • The authors suggest this could one day help contain these tumors before surgery.
  • This is animal work only.

Need to know: Invasion into healthy brain is what makes GBM so hard to remove completely, so a way to hold it in place is an interesting angle even though it's very early.

Keto updates

Ketone drink lowered the hunger hormone ghrelin compared with a sugar drink

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • After an overnight fast, people drank either a ketone supplement or a calorie-matched sugar drink.
  • Blood ketones rose from 0.2 to 3.3 mM with the ketone drink.
  • Ghrelin and reported hunger were lower 2 to 4 hours later.
  • The study is Stubbs et al., Obesity 2018.

Need to know: It supports the common experience that being in deeper ketosis makes hunger easier to manage.

Very low salt raised insulin resistance in healthy adults

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • 152 healthy adults spent one week on very low salt and one week on high salt.
  • Insulin resistance was higher after the low-salt week.
  • Aldosterone was about six times higher and norepinephrine rose.

Need to know: Keto already raises salt needs, and this is another reason not to cut salt too hard.

Lifting helps the brain partly through metabolism

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Regular strength and aerobic exercise were linked to better brain function in middle age, and women who lifted had faster reaction times.
  • Bikman's point is that muscle takes up most glucose when insulin rises, so more used muscle means less insulin needed per meal.
  • Separately, combining a GLP-1 drug with exercise kept off about twice as much body fat.

Need to know: Muscle is a metabolic organ, which makes resistance training a lever for glucose and insulin control.
