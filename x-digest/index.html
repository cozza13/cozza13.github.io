1. Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Bizaxofusp (MDNA55) numbers are out: median OS 13.7 vs 6.2 months in unresectable recurrent IDH-wildtype GBM

For you 10 | Unique 9

  • Medicenna released the SNO 2026 Phase 2b data this morning. It was presented in an oral session by a UCSF neuro-oncologist.
  • Single high-dose treatment (at least 180 micrograms), confirmed IDH-wildtype, N=19: median overall survival 13.7 months vs 6.2 months for a propensity-matched external control (HR 0.499, p=0.013). 12-month survival 52.6% vs 15.6%; 24-month 26.3% vs 6.7%.
  • Broader groups held up but with a smaller gap: all IDH-wildtype per-protocol (N=37) 12.5 vs 7.3 months (HR 0.625).
  • The control arm (80 registry records) came from UCSF and St. Michael's Hospital. The company itself notes the comparison is small and retrospective, and not a substitute for randomized evidence.
  • Next step: a pivotal hybrid randomized Phase 3 at 240 micrograms, with partners still being sought.

Need to know: This is the number the SNO watch had been waiting for. It is a single-arm study compared with an external control, so it is Emerging evidence, not proof; the Phase 3 is what will settle it.

IN8bio gamma-delta T cells in newly diagnosed GBM: median OS 20.9 months with repeat dosing

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • Final INB-200/INB-400 data from an SNO oral session (press release Nov 16): repeat-dose patients had median OS 20.9 months and median PFS 13.0 months, vs 13.2 and 6.6 months in a small standard-of-care control group.
  • 7 of 14 repeat-dose patients were alive at two years, vs 1 of 10 controls.
  • The cells are engineered to resist temozolomide, so they can be given during chemo. No cytokine release syndrome, neurotoxicity or treatment-related serious adverse events reported.
  • Very small, non-randomized (17 treated patients across UAB, Ohio State, Moffitt and Cleveland Clinic).

Need to know: Another small early-phase signal in newly diagnosed GBM. Worth tracking, but the comparison group is tiny.

Other cancer

Annals of Oncology: ctDNA-guided adjuvant immunotherapy in mismatch-repair-deficient tumors

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Phase II: 23 of 184 patients were ctDNA-positive after surgery; 6 of 19 relapsed before treatment could start.
  • Pembrolizumab (n=13) cleared ctDNA in 85%, but recurrence was still 38%.
  • Commentators' line: molecular clearance is not the same as clinical benefit.

Need to know: A useful caution for any liquid-biopsy result: clearing a blood marker does not yet prove better outcomes.

3. Keto updates

Review of 361 studies: eating less protein may slow aging, with methionine as one driver

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • University of Wisconsin-Madison scientists reviewed how protein affects metabolism and lifespan from flies and mice to humans (summary post).
  • In animals, lower protein extended lifespan at the same calories; methionine, isoleucine and valine seem to drive it, partly through the hormone FGF21.
  • Human data are more limited: recent trials of cutting protein lowered weight and body fat and improved fasting glucose.

Need to know: Lines up with your lower-methionine approach. Human outcome data are thin, so this is mainly a mechanism story. Secondary summary; find the review itself before citing it.
