Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

UCLA trial: a combination of two immunotherapies drew more immune cells into recurrent glioblastoma

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • UCLA Neuro says a UCLA-led trial (Drs. Timothy Cloughesy, Robert Prins and colleagues) tested two immunotherapies combined in recurrent glioblastoma.
  • The combination brought more immune cells into the tumors.
  • Patients with more of these tumor-fighting cells tended to have better survival.
  • The team frames it as a clue to what makes some brain tumors more responsive to immunotherapy.

Need to know: This comes from an institutional post, and 'tended to have better survival' is a correlation, not a proven benefit. Worth reading the linked write-up for the agents and trial design.

Keto updates

First results from the NQDIT ketogenic metabolic therapy RCT in schizophrenia and bipolar disorder

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • Baszucki Group says first results from the North Queensland Dietary Intervention Trial are out in Frontiers in Nutrition.
  • It is a randomized controlled trial of ketogenic metabolic therapy in schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder and bipolar disorder.
  • Dr. Matt Calkins describes this paper as feasibility data, reporting 'great tolerability, good ketones'.
  • The primary-outcome paper is still forthcoming.

Need to know: This adds randomized evidence that people can stay on KMT and reach ketosis. That supports adherence, though the main efficacy results aren't out yet.

@BaszuckiGroup · @MattCalkinsMD

Bikman: ketones (BHB) alongside glucose for low blood sugar in type 1 diabetes

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • Ben Bikman notes that the standard treatment for low blood sugar in type 1 diabetes is glucose.
  • He cites a small crossover study in adults with T1D that gave a drink of glucose plus BHB.
  • He calls the evidence that ketones help as well 'fascinating'.

Need to know: It's a small study, but it fits the idea that the brain runs well on ketones when glucose is low, which is relevant to living at a low GKI. Read the study before generalizing.

@BenBikmanPhD

Fat cell biology: large fat cells predict diabetes, and weight loss shrinks fat cells without cutting their number

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Bikman: among adults with normal glucose tolerance, those with the largest belly fat cells at baseline were far more likely to develop type 2 diabetes.
  • Roy: in a 2008 Karolinska study, 20 people lost 18% of their weight after gastric banding. Fat cells shrank by about a third, but the count held at roughly 84 to 85 billion.
  • About 1 in 10 fat cells is replaced each year (from bomb-pulse carbon dating), and Science devoted this week's issue to GLP-1 drugs and fat tissue.

Need to know: Where fat sits and how big the cells are matter more than the scale number. This is useful context for maintaining 190 lb without chasing weight loss.

@BenBikmanPhD · @agingroy
