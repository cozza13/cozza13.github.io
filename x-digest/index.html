Preclinical GBM research: two-target CAR-T, stroke and glioma growth, and space-grown organoids

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Researchers built GPNMB-targeting CAR-T cells that attack both glioblastoma cells and the tumor-supporting myeloid cells around them, with long-term tumor control in experimental models.
  • Another study found a prior stroke changes astrocyte calcium signaling and recruits tumor-supporting immune cells, speeding glioma progression in animal models.
  • A PNAS study found glioblastoma organoids grown on the International Space Station looked more like human tumors, including a more immune-suppressing environment, than Earth-grown versions.
  • All of this is lab and animal work.

Need to know: The common thread is the tumor's surroundings: immune and support cells are increasingly the target, not just the cancer cells themselves.

Repurposed-drug claims keep circulating: loperamide again, and a rat study of nasal ivermectin

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • The 2021 Goethe University lab-dish finding that loperamide (Imodium) triggers death in glioblastoma cells went viral again on several accounts.
  • A separate rat study reported that ivermectin in nanocapsules delivered through the nose cut implanted glioblastoma volume by about 70% versus untreated controls over 10 days.
  • In that study, free ivermectin and other formulations did not give comparable reductions, so the delivery system seems to be doing the work.
  • Some posts pair these with broad 'ivermectin and mebendazole cured my patients' claims that aren't backed by trials.

Need to know: Interesting delivery science in rats, but nowhere near human evidence; worth recognizing these when they get forwarded.

Other oncology

Personalized mRNA cancer vaccine: phase 3 melanoma win, and a lab offering custom builds

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Moderna and Merck's INTerpath-001 trial (1,137 people with fully removed stage IIB to IV melanoma) tested a vaccine built from each patient's tumor mutations plus Keytruda, presented at ESMO 2026.
  • The vaccine is not approved yet.
  • A lab in Hyderabad (RNAFoundry) says it can design and make one for a patient's medical team in 8 to 10 weeks for about $300,000.
  • The post was written by @agingroy, who also linked the trial details.

Need to know: Personalized tumor-mutation vaccines are moving from trial to (expensive) private manufacturing faster than regulators; brain tumors aren't part of this trial.

ESMO follow-up: tarlatamab shows a long-term survival tail in small-cell lung cancer

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • At 22 months of follow-up in DeLLphi-304, 30% of patients on tarlatamab were alive at 2 years versus 15% on chemo.
  • Sustained disease control for at least 52 weeks was seen in 24.4% versus 4.3%.
  • The results were published in Annals of Oncology and presented at ESMO 2026.
  • Oncologists see this as T-cell engagers producing the kind of durable 'tail' seen with checkpoint drugs.

Need to know: A clear sign T-cell engager drugs can produce lasting responses in a hard solid tumor.

Ketogenic diet research beyond weight: Parkinson's trial and a 72-hour fasting claim

For you 6 | Unique 5

  • A randomized crossover trial in 52 people with Parkinson's compared 8 weeks of a Mediterranean-ketogenic diet with an MCT-supplemented Mediterranean diet; neither markedly changed the gut microbiome.
  • A widely shared post says a 72-hour fast can 'reset' the immune system; that's based on older mouse and small human work and is overstated.
  • Nick Norwitz posted a nuanced take on the FLOWPROMOTE trial, where lipid-lowering drugs regressed plaque in high-risk patients, stressing individual risk-benefit.

Need to know: Keto keeps being studied for brain conditions; the microbiome result was neutral, not negative.
