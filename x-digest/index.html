1. Cancer updates

Glioblastoma

Two Nature papers map how glioblastoma cells shift states

For you 9 | Unique 8

  • EpigenomeTech flagged two new Nature papers from the Bayraktar and Stegle labs (with Moritz Mall and Sam Behjati).
  • One decodes the gene-regulatory programs behind glioblastoma plasticity; the other describes a spatiotemporal cancer-cell trajectory underlying tumor heterogeneity.
  • Both use single-cell multiomics to show how GBM cells move between states rather than staying fixed.

Need to know: Plasticity is a big reason GBM resists single-target therapy. These are basic-science maps, not treatments, but they are the kind of foundation future combination strategies get built on.

Cancer Cell: platelet PAR4 signaling shapes sex differences in GBM immunity

For you 7 | Unique 8

  • A new Cancer Cell study reports that platelet PAR4 signaling suppresses CD8 T-cell activity in glioblastoma.
  • Blocking PAR4 improved survival in female mice only, an estrogen-dependent effect linked to estrogen receptor beta.
  • Preclinical mouse work, summarized by a reagent vendor.

Need to know: An unusual angle on GBM immunology: blood platelets and sex hormones influencing T-cell response. Early-stage, but worth noting as a mechanism.
