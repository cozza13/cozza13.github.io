Stanford's RABIT model infers blood proteomes from health records and flags a CMV-linked signal in brain tumors

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • Sci Transl Med (Nov 25), Aghaeepour lab at Stanford: RABIT turns a patient's longitudinal EHR history into an estimated plasma-proteome profile. It was trained on patients with both EHRs and measured proteomics, then validated in a separate cohort.
  • Adding the inferred profiles improved 5-year disease-onset prediction across 14 conditions (n = 449,223; mean adjusted AUPRC +33.7%). In an external cohort (n = 1,038,419) the gain was +70.3%.
  • It also beat established clinical predictors for CKD progression, major cardiac events, and response to anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy in metastatic NSCLC.
  • Brain tumor angle: the authors scaled small-cohort signatures up to population datasets and generated hypotheses linking cytomegalovirus (CMV)-associated immune states to variation in brain tumor outcomes. @EricTopol highlighted it as "a CMV 9-protein signature associated with brain tumor risk."

Need to know: CMV in glioblastoma has been a contested thread for years. This adds a population-scale signal, but the proteins are inferred from records, not measured, and the authors call it hypothesis-generating. Evidence: Emerging.

Other cancer

NEJM: OptiTROP-Lung06 pairs a TROP2 ADC with pembrolizumab and more than doubles PFS in PD-L1-negative NSCLC

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Phase 3, 455 patients with untreated advanced nonsquamous NSCLC, PD-L1 TPS <1% and no targetable alterations. Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT) + pembrolizumab vs platinum-pemetrexed + pembrolizumab.
  • Median PFS 15.0 vs 5.7 months (HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.36-0.65).
  • ORR was similar (50.2% vs 47.3%), but median response duration was 13.6 vs 6.8 months.
  • OS HR 0.58 is immature and descriptive, not confirmatory (per @youngkwangchae's summary).

Need to know: An ADC could replace chemo as immunotherapy's partner in a hard-to-treat lung cancer group. The gain is durability, not response rate. Evidence: Established for PFS (phase 3); OS still pending.

2. AI updates

Anthropic moved its Messages API from Python to Rust, using production traffic as the verifier for Claude agents

For you 7 | Unique 8

  • Anthropic's engineering post (Nov 25) says API traffic grew about 1,800x from early 2024 to fall 2026. Claude wrote a first Rust version of the core request path in about a week.
  • The hard part was checking parity: the Python API kept changing, model outputs are nondeterministic, and engineers can't read customer requests.
  • They turned the live Python API into a verifier, compared it with the Rust version on production traffic, and automatically sent agents to fix the differences they found.
  • Result: about 70% less CPU per request and about 20% faster median time to first token, with no downtime and no feature freeze. Public traffic moved to Rust in September 2026.

Need to know: A concrete playbook for agent-driven rewrites of live systems: put human judgment into what counts as correct, and let agents handle the rest. It's useful for anyone building multi-agent tooling.

@AnthropicAI · Engineering post

3. Keto updates

KetoSAge 'PIT protocol': 7 days of paired pre-dinner glucose and BHB to define a 'EU-GKI' state

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • @I_mitochondria (Isabella Cooper, PhD) lays out the KetoSAge framework: sustained low insulin demand keeps insulin below a personal hyperinsulinaemia threshold (PIT).
  • The protocol pairs capillary glucose and beta-hydroxybutyrate on at least 7 consecutive days, before dinner and at least 3 h after lunch.
  • The criterion is met when BHB is at least 0.5 mmol/L or GKI is 10 or lower on each day. That defines the 'EU-GKI' phenotype: normal glucose with sustained ketonaemia and normal insulin.
  • She cites the KetoSAge SHBG/sex-hormone study and the GKI biomarker paper.

Need to know: A functional, fingerstick-only way to define sustained ketosis, built on the same paired glucose and ketone readings many keto trackers already log. The thresholds are the framework's own and not a clinical standard. Evidence: Emerging.

@I_mitochondria

4. General news

1Password breach: one employee's GitHub account led to stolen customer records and source code. Vaults weren't touched

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • 1Password disclosed on Nov 25 that it began investigating on Nov 19. An attacker used an employee's GitHub account to inject backend code that downloaded customer account records.
  • Taken: names, emails, account and user IDs, sign-in timestamps, public keys, SRP parameters, and invitation, provisioning and recovery tokens. Internal source code and CI/CD access were also exposed.
  • 1Password says vaults and encryption keys were not compromised and it isn't requiring password or Secret Key changes. Verification codes went from 6 digits to 10 alphanumeric characters.
  • The main risk now is phishing that uses the breach as a lure. Go to the app or site directly rather than clicking emailed links.

Need to know: If you use 1Password, watch for unexpected invite or recovery emails. It's also a reminder to lock down GitHub tokens and require reviewed merges for anything that deploys.
