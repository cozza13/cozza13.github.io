SNO 2026 top abstract: LLMs turn messy clinical reports into a neuro-oncology research database

For you 9 | Unique 8

Recurrent GBM: ImmunityBio shows Anktiva + PD-L1 t-haNK cells (QUILT-3.078) at SNO

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Dr. Simon Khagi presented QUILT-3.078 in a rapid oral session: ImmunityBio's IL-15 agonist Anktiva (N-803) combined with PD-L1-targeted natural killer cells (t-haNK) in recurrent glioblastoma.
  • The company post gave no response or survival numbers.
  • This is an investigational combination in an early trial.

Need to know: One more immunotherapy approach for recurrent GBM. Wait for the actual data before reading much into it.

NYU: whole-genome sequencing of tumor DNA in spinal fluid to follow high-grade glioma

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Alexandra Miller (NYU Langone) discussed whole-genome sequencing of circulating tumor DNA in cerebrospinal fluid from high-grade glioma patients (OncLive video).

Vanderbilt: IL-8 in GBM switches immune cells into suppressors

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • A Cancer Immunology Research paper (online Nov 12) from Vanderbilt and CU Anschutz finds that IL-8 in human glioblastoma pushes myeloid immune cells into an immunosuppressive state.
  • Laboratory and tissue work; it suggests IL-8 as a possible target for helping immunotherapy work.

Need to know: Helps explain why GBM is "cold" to immunotherapy. Early-stage science.

Other cancer

FDA rejects ivonescimab for EGFR-mutant lung cancer

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • STAT's Adam Feuerstein reports the FDA turned down Summit's ivonescimab (a PD-1 x VEGF bispecific antibody) in EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer.
  • His summary: no survival benefit, no approval.
  • Ivonescimab was one of the most hyped new oncology drugs of the last two years.

Need to know: The FDA is still asking for overall survival, not just progression-free survival. That bar applies to brain tumor drugs too.

Feuerstein

Norwitz: "high-fat diet" mouse studies usually use soybean oil, lard and sugar

For you 7 | Unique 5

  • Nick Norwitz reminds low-carb eaters that "high-fat diet causes cancer" headlines often come from mice fed a chow of soybean oil, lard and sucrose.
  • 669 likes; a useful lens for reading diet-and-cancer headlines.

Need to know: Check what the "high-fat diet" actually was before applying a study to a whole-food keto diet.

Pembrolizumab beats chemo in dMMR endometrial cancer (KEYNOTE-C93, Lancet)

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Phase 3 KEYNOTE-C93: in mismatch-repair-deficient advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer, pembrolizumab gave median progression-free survival of 38.2 months vs 8.4 with carboplatin-paclitaxel.
  • A possible chemo-free first-line option for that subgroup.

Need to know: Another example of a biomarker-selected group doing far better on immunotherapy alone.
