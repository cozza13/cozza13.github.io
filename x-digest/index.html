Glioblastoma

Candel's oncolytic virus (linoserpaturev) posts updated phase 1b survival numbers in recurrent high-grade glioma

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Bioupdatesdaily summarizes updated phase 1b data from Candel Therapeutics ($CADL) on linoserpaturev, an engineered herpes virus injected into the tumor.
  • In arms A and B (50 patients with recurrent high-grade glioma or glioblastoma), median overall survival was 12.0 months, with some patients alive at 18 to 48 months.
  • In arm C (repeat dosing) median survival was 14.3 months, but that figure is still immature.
  • Biotech watcher KPDonahueX flagged Candel's glioblastoma data as one of the SITC conference releases this weekend.

Need to know: This is a small, single-arm early study, so there's no control group to compare against. It's worth watching what Candel presents in full and whether it moves into a randomized trial.

@Bioupdatesdaily · @KPDonahueX

Other cancer

Immunotherapy shows a signal in 'cold' mismatch-repair-proficient colon cancer (UNICORN phase II)

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Dr. Dirican shared a Nature Medicine phase II trial of botensilimab plus balstilimab given before surgery.
  • In mismatch-repair-proficient (pMMR) tumors, 36% had a major pathologic response and 29% a complete one.
  • In mismatch-repair-deficient tumors, 13 of 14 had a complete response.
  • The poster stresses that the sample is small and phase III is needed.

Need to know: pMMR colon cancer usually ignores checkpoint drugs, so any response here is notable, but it's a small study.

Mouse study: a ketogenic diet may feed the immune cells fighting cancer, which complicates the glutamine worry

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Cary Kelly highlighted a new mouse study on cancer and ketones.
  • It starts from the common question of whether glutamine in protein fuels cancer.
  • The point is that immune cells fighting the tumor also need fuel, and the study suggests a ketogenic diet may help them.
  • Omega-3 (DHA) mouse work shared by changmyung1981 also found lower inflammatory markers (IL-1β down about threefold) and fat-tissue changes without weight loss.

Need to know: Both are mouse studies, so they're mechanism hints, not human evidence. The immune angle is relevant to anyone thinking about keto alongside immunotherapy, and the paper is worth pulling.
