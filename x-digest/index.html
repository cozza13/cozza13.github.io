Anktiva plus CAR-NK plus bevacizumab in recurrent GBM: lymphocytes up, survival not clearly

For you 8 | Unique 8

  • A SNO 2026 abstract reported ImmunityBio's Anktiva (an IL-15 agonist) with CAR-NK cells and bevacizumab in recurrent glioblastoma.
  • Per a detailed critique by @andrewcaravello, lymphocyte counts rose but median survival was about 9.5 months.
  • His argument: total lymphocyte count is a gauge, and the abstract reports no CD4 or naive T-cell subsets, which are the cells GBM treatment depletes.
  • The critique comes from an investor account with positions in the space, so read the abstract itself before weighing it.

Need to know: A useful reminder that raising a blood marker is not the same as changing outcomes. Wait for the full abstract before reading much into it.

Broader oncology

AI-derived "ClinicalAge" from health records tracks disease and cancer risk

For you 7 | Unique 7

  • Eric Topol highlighted a Lancet Digital Health paper deriving a "clinical age" from longitudinal electronic health records.
  • It diverges widely from chronological age and correlates with risk of many diseases and cancers across UK Biobank and a second large cohort.

Need to know: A close cousin of commercial biological-age scores; interesting as a framework, not something you can order.

Topol post

Location of the target, not just its level, may predict ADC benefit

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • A Nature Medicine study used AI pathology to measure the membrane-to-cytoplasm ratio of TROP2 in lung cancer.
  • That ratio, rather than standard IHC staining, predicted benefit from TROP2 antibody-drug conjugates in the TROPION-Lung trials.

Need to know: A general trend: AI pathology is starting to pick who benefits from targeted drugs, which could reach brain tumors too.

3. Keto updates

Blood ketones follow a daily rhythm, per 11.8 million Keto-Mojo readings

For you 9 | Unique 9

  • Keto-Mojo and Adrian Soto-Mota analyzed more than 11.8 million de-identified blood ketone and glucose readings with timestamps.
  • Blood BHB shows a circadian pattern; Ben Bikman notes ketones, like glucose, respond to hormones that cycle through the day.
  • Dave Feldman flagged it as a first at this scale.

Need to know: Directly relevant to anyone tracking ketones or GKI: the time of day you test changes the number, so testing at a consistent time matters for comparing days.

Keto-Mojo · Feldman · Bikman

Virta study: ketosis-targeted care linked to fewer heart events

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • A propensity-matched claims study in Cardiology and Therapy compared about 4,877 people per group (mean age 51, no prior heart disease).
  • Virta's ketosis-targeted remote care was associated with a 56% lower rate of major cardiovascular events (MACE-3 hazard ratio 0.44).
  • Shawn Baker, Tim Noakes and Metabolic Mind's Bret Scher all highlighted it; it is observational, so it shows association, not proof.

Need to know: Useful counterweight to the "keto equals heart risk" worry for long-term ketogenic eating.

Study summary · Baker · Scher thread

Bikman: one short night of sleep makes you measurably insulin resistant

For you 8 | Unique 6

  • Healthy adults who slept four hours instead of a full night had clearly worse insulin action the next afternoon, including in the liver (Donga et al., 2010).
  • In a separate post he notes infused BHB reduced leucine burning in muscle, meaning ketones help spare protein.

Need to know: Backs up sleep as a core metabolic lever alongside ketosis, and the protein-sparing note suits moderate-protein keto.

Sleep post · Ketones and protein

CGM vs lab A1c can disagree, and why

For you 6 | Unique 6

  • Dr. David Unwin showed a case where a lab A1c was falsely high because red blood cells were living longer, while the CGM was right.
  • A NEJM trial found CGMs gave non-insulin type 2 diabetics about 5 more hours a day in target range.
  • Keto-Mojo spoke at the Ketogenic Dietary Therapies 2026 meeting in Salzburg.

Need to know: A good reason to read A1c alongside CGM or meter data rather than on its own.
