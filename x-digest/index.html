For you 7 | Unique 7

Other oncology

Eliglustat (Gaucher drug) kills cancer cells by jamming lysosomes

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • A VCU Massey team (Can Senkal) reports in Cell Death Discovery that eliglustat (Cerdelga), a glucosylceramide synthase inhibitor, killed several cancer cell lines.
  • The mechanism was lysosome failure rather than ceramide build-up; restarting lysosome production rescued the cells.
  • Because the drug is already approved, it could move into trials faster. So far this is cell-line work only.

Need to know: This is a repurposing lead in sphingolipid and lysosome metabolism, a theme next to the metabolic angle. It is preclinical.

3. Keto updates

Keto therapy cut seizure-medication load in drug-resistant epilepsy

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • A clinical cohort of 56 people (34 children, 22 adults) on ketogenic diet therapy for at least 6 months: 64.3% reduced or stopped at least one anti-seizure medication.
  • Adults' average medication load fell about 24.5%.
  • This was done inside a supervised program; the post stresses never cutting medications without a care team.

Need to know: Relevant to brain metabolism and ketogenic therapy as an adjunct, with seizure medications a familiar part of GBM care. It is a cohort study, not an RCT.
