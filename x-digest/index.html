First in vivo CAR-T data from CARsgen (CT1182): CAR T cells made inside the patient

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • @dr_dirican: CT1182 is a CD19/CD20 in vivo CAR-T, with no ex vivo manufacturing and no lymphodepleting chemo.
  • Early results, n=6: ORR 83% (3 CR, 2 PR), median follow-up 3.2 months.
  • Only grade 1 CRS; no ICANS reported.

Need to know: In vivo CAR-T could make cell therapy faster and cheaper. This is blood cancer, six patients, very short follow-up (Emerging).

2. AI updates

Anthropic posts a Claude-written proof that VP ≠ VNP, the algebraic cousin of P vs NP

For you 7 | Unique 9

  • @MTSlive: Anthropic posted a 70-page paper to its formal-math GitHub repo crediting an internal research Claude model as sole author.
  • The claim: the permanent and the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial need superpolynomial-size circuits (Valiant's 1979 conjecture), for every field (the permanent case excludes characteristic 2).
  • A Lean 4 formal proof (~73,000 lines, per a post @MatthewBerman quoted) was checked by Lean's kernel plus an independent checker.
  • No human mathematician has checked it yet, and Anthropic says it doesn't settle P vs NP. The Polymarket post drew 631K views.

Need to know: If it holds up, it's the strongest example yet of an AI producing a major, machine-verified math result. Treat it as a claim until human experts weigh in.

@MTSlive explainer · @Polymarket

3. Keto updates

LDL day: Topol calls it the one surrogate we can count on, ORION-4 reports outcomes, and Bikman points to TG/HDL

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • @EricTopol (31K views): "Targeting LDL cholesterol is still the only surrogate cardiovascular endpoint we can count on."
  • @agingroy: in the Oxford-led ORION-4 trial (16,000+ people), twice-yearly inclisiran cut heart attacks and strokes; until now it was proven only to halve LDL.
  • @BenBikmanPhD: fasting triglycerides ≥ ~130 mg/dL or a TG/HDL ratio ≥ ~3 flags insulin resistance about as well as full metabolic-syndrome criteria; he argues LDL alone predicts poorly.
  • The two camps posted hours apart.

Need to know: The lipid debate keeps splitting along the same line. ORION-4 strengthens the LDL-lowering case with hard outcomes (Established once published in full); Bikman's TG/HDL point is a marker of insulin resistance, not a substitute for outcome data.

@EricTopol · @agingroy · @BenBikmanPhD
