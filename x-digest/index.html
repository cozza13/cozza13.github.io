Glioblastoma

FDA clears Alpha Tau to double its US recurrent glioblastoma trial (REGAIN), with new data at SNO in November

For you 8 | Unique 7

  • Alpha Tau (DRTS) says the FDA approved expanding its Alpha DaRT trial in recurrent GBM from 10 to 20 patients.
  • Alpha DaRT places tiny alpha-radiation sources directly inside the tumor.
  • The amended protocol adds progression-free survival and quality-of-life measures, and the company says it may support a market submission.
  • New trial data will be presented at the SNO 2026 annual meeting on Nov 13.
  • A finance account (@MonkofFinance) noted the first three patients had 100% local disease control; that's a company-reported early number, not a controlled result.

Need to know: A small device trial, but it's one of the few US recurrent-GBM studies moving forward on the regulatory side this week. The SNO presentation on Nov 13 is the date to watch for real numbers.

UCLA: clues to making immunotherapy work better against glioblastoma, plus a new mouse model that mirrors human GBM

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • The Cancer Letter highlighted UCLA Jonsson Cancer Center work on why immunotherapy usually fails in GBM and what might help it work.
  • Separately, a UCLA team (Liau, Prins, Nathanson and others) published a syngeneic mouse glioma model in Cancer Research on Oct 13.

  • The post only gives the headline, so the specific immunotherapy findings aren't in today's feed.

Need to know: Better mouse models are a big deal in GBM because many treatments that work in older models fail in people. Worth opening the Cancer Letter link for the details.

Preclinical GBM roundup: CAR-T before surgery, intranasal ivermectin nanocapsules, and a cholesterol-storage target

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • University of Geneva researchers found giving CAR-T cells before surgery improved tumor control in mouse GBM models.
  • An engineered ivermectin nanocapsule given through the nose cut glioma volume about 70% in rats after 10 days; both @pharmacyinusa and @mcgill_medicine stressed this is rat data, not proof in people.
  • @RoryPopert's group is repurposing an existing drug to block SOAT1 (ACAT1), a cholesterol-storage enzyme GBM cells rely on.

Need to know: All early-stage animal or lab work. The SOAT1 and cholesterol angle overlaps with the metabolic side of GBM research you follow.

Other oncology

ESMO 2026 previews: rectal cancer immunotherapy win, a null esophageal trial, and the Phase 3s to watch

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • UNION (Phase 3): short-course radiation then camrelizumab plus chemo in locally advanced rectal cancer reached 91.3% three-year event-free survival versus 77.4% (HR 0.33), though the arms also differed in radiation type.
  • KEYNOTE-975: adding pembrolizumab to chemoradiation in esophageal cancer showed no benefit (EFS HR 0.97, OS HR 1.00 in CPS 10 or higher).
  • @ozdogan_md lists five positive Phase 3 toplines to watch in Madrid: INTerpath-001, VOLGA, DeLLphi-305, KRAScendo 1 and SAFFRON.
  • @OncoAlert posted its GU top picks, including VOLGA, KEYNOTE-B15, CAPItello-281 and LITESPARK-011/012.

Need to know: ESMO runs Oct 23 to 27. INTerpath-001 is the personalized mRNA vaccine readout, the one most relevant to future brain-tumor vaccine work.

Ivonescimab beats durvalumab on survival in biliary tract cancer (HARMONi-GI1, sponsor-reported)

For you 6 | Unique 7

  • Phase 3 HARMONi-GI1 compared ivonescimab (a PD-1 and VEGF bispecific) plus chemo against durvalumab plus chemo in advanced biliary tract cancer.
  • The sponsor reports it met overall survival superiority, along with progression-free survival and response rate.
  • Detailed efficacy and safety data haven't been released yet.

Need to know: Ivonescimab keeps winning head-to-head against established immunotherapies. Its VEGF-blocking half is interesting for brain tumors, where VEGF drives a lot of the swelling.

Bikman on metabolism and the brain: prediabetes and depression, ketones in infants, and insulin after gestational diabetes

For you 7 | Unique 6

  • In a Dutch cohort of 601 adults without depression, those who developed prediabetes over 2 years had more than 2.5 times the risk of later major depression.
  • He notes healthy breastfed infants are normally in mild ketosis, with ketones fueling the fast-growing brain.
  • A new study of 178 South Asian and 107 Nordic women after gestational diabetes found the South Asian women secreted more insulin even with normal glucose, which he says shows high insulin and insulin resistance travel together.
  • Observational data throughout.

Need to know: More support for watching insulin, not just glucose, as a metabolic marker.
